# Product

## Register
brand (marketing site, Persuade mode)

## Users
Senior decision-makers aged 40 to 60 (executives, C-level, HR and L&D leads) who visit to judge credibility and arrange team training or an institutional partnership. Secondary: adult learners preparing for UK higher education, and current learners who need Moodle. They read carefully on laptops, often at a desk between meetings; many wear reading glasses. Some will arrive on a phone.

## Product Purpose
City Language Hub (part of City Language School, Planet Education Network) offers Academic Learning Platform (ALP), Corporate Training Services (CTS), a two-week Academic Readiness Programme and Employability and Professional Pathways, delivered through guided Moodle learning. The site explains the pathways, routes learners to Moodle ("Learner Login") and routes organisations to an enquiry. Source: City Language Hub Website Wording Plan (5 Oct 2026) and Visual Mockups 1.

## Brand Personality
Assured, calm, precise. Institutional without being stiff. British English, sentence-light, no hype.

## Anti-references
Startup-purple gradients, stock-photo collages, claims of guaranteed admission, employment or unlimited support, tiny grey text, dense icon-card grids, cookie-cutter SaaS.

## Design Principles
1. Credibility first: restraint, real structure, no superlatives.
2. Readable at a glance for 40 to 60 year olds: 18px body, strong contrast, big targets.
3. Two clear doors on every page: Learner Login and Ask About a Programme / Discuss Team Training.
4. Use the programme names exactly as the wording plan sets them.

## Assumptions (no interview was possible)
- Stakeholder mockup colour DNA (aubergine, violet, lavender / amber / mint phase tints) is kept; layout and type are redesigned.
- Learner Login URL is a placeholder until the verified Moodle address is supplied.
- No people photography is supplied at usable resolution; the prototype uses authored UI instead.
